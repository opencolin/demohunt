# Demo Source Map

Candidate video sources for sourcing real startup/founder demos. Each item is unchecked until ingested and verified per the v0 data contract (`verified:true` + real `batch` + working video ID).

## YC channel

- Main channel: <https://www.youtube.com/@ycombinator>

### YC company directory URL pattern

```
https://www.ycombinator.com/companies?batch={batch}
```

> **NOTE:** YC does NOT publish per-batch Demo Day playlists on its YouTube channel — Demo Day is investor-only / not livestreamed publicly. No W23/S23/W24/S24/F24/W25/X25/S25/F25/W26 Demo Day playlists exist on @ycombinator. The closest public batch demo content is older one-off uploads (e.g. S19 Demo Day) and per-company pitch videos. Use ycombinator.com/launches for current-batch product demo videos instead.

## Extra sources

- [ ] **Techstars** — <https://www.youtube.com/@techstars> — Strongest non-YC source for real startup demo pitches. Official channel publicly posts full Demo Day pitch videos from cohorts worldwide (NYC/Boulder/London/etc). Unlike YC these Demo Days ARE public. See playlist Demo Day Events list=PLbMZj2MrUb5w6NuqyswHHeOBWmuC3Odsv.
- [ ] **a16z Speedrun** — <https://www.youtube.com/@a16zspeedrun> — Dedicated channel for a16z's accelerator (Speedrun). Hosts Demo Day presentations (2-min founder pitches; SR003/004/005 cohorts) and talks previously private to founders. High-quality AI/games/tech demos.
- [ ] **AI Engineer (World's Fair / Summit)** — <https://www.youtube.com/@aiDotEngineer> — High-production keynotes and live product demos from AI startups and labs; 15M+ views. Per-event/per-track playlists for 2024 and 2025 fairs. Excellent for AI product demos.
- [ ] **Entrepreneur First (EF)** — <https://www.youtube.com/@Join_ef> — Global talent-investor accelerator. Public 'EF Demo Day Highlights' playlist list=PLaDjE1VTVKMQjrTDF9WZN8bPOboqqhjHg with 3-min cohort founder pitches from London/SF/etc.
- [ ] **TechCrunch Startup Battlefield (Disrupt)** — <https://www.youtube.com/@TechCrunch> — Original startup pitch+demo competition (found Dropbox/Discord/Cloudflare). Many individual Battlefield pitch videos per year; classic on-stage demo format. Search 'Startup Battlefield' on the channel.
- [ ] **LAUNCH Accelerator (Jason Calacanis / This Week in Startups)** — <https://www.youtube.com/@ThisWeekIn> — Public Demo Day episodes (e.g. Accelerator 30/32) with ~7 startup pitches each plus investor Q&A. Consistent, recurring, fully public demo pitches.
- [ ] **Slush** — <https://www.youtube.com/@SlushHQ> — Helsinki conference. Slush 100 pitch competition (winner gets EUR 1M investment) finals and founder demos posted publicly.
- [ ] **Product Hunt** — <https://www.producthunt.com> — Not just YouTube — product pages embed maker-submitted demo videos at massive scale across all categories. Best bulk source of short product demos outside YouTube. (Channel: youtube.com/@ProductHunt for interviews.)
- [ ] **Vercel** — <https://www.youtube.com/@VercelHQ> — Ship conference + Launch Week product demo videos; high-quality AI/web product showcases (v0, AI SDK, etc).
- [ ] **Supabase** — <https://www.youtube.com/@Supabase> — Launch Week new-product demo videos every cycle; developer/AI tooling focused, consistently polished demos.
